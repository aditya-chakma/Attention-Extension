Attention Insights Settings

Blocked sites

Blocked sites (and their subdomains) are stopped before they load. Give a site a schedule to block it only at certain times.

    Shorts & Reels

    Remove the endless-scroll sections while you browse. Opening a Short or a Reel takes you to the YouTube or Facebook home page instead.

    Unblock PIN

    With a PIN set, a blocked page offers to lift the block for a few minutes. It is a speed bump for your own impulses, not a lock: you can reset it here at any time without knowing it.

    Usage statistics

    The Insights page counts removed Shorts and Reels, blocked visits, temporary unblocks and screen time, kept on this device for 180 days.
